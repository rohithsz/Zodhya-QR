🧪 QR Code Tester

Paste any URL and a QR will render below. Scan it with your phone to test.

Make it scannable from your phone

A QR pointing to a file:// path on your laptop won't open on a phone. Two ways to make it work:

Option A — GitHub Pages (recommended)

  1. Push this folder to GitHub and enable Pages (see repo README).
  2. Paste your live URL above (e.g. https://you.github.io/zodhya-event-kit/rohith.html) and scan.

Option B — Local Wi-Fi server

  1. In PowerShell, from this folder: python -m http.server 8000
  2. Find your laptop's Wi-Fi IP: (Get-NetIPAddress -AddressFamily IPv4 | ? InterfaceAlias -match 'Wi-Fi').IPAddress
  3. Type http://<that-ip>:8000/rohith.html above. Phone must be on the same Wi-Fi.
